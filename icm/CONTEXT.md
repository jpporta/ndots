# ICM umbrella — route configuration changes
One job: provide the contracts, references, templates, and records for configuration changes.

## Inputs
- Working: one copied folder under `changes/`.
- Reference: `_shared/targets.md`, `_shared/safety.md`, and `_shared/external-sources.md` when relevant.
- Do NOT load: unrelated change folders, historical OpenSpec material, or external dotfiles unless the change plan names them.

## Process
1. Copy `_templates/change/` into `changes/YYYY-MM-DD-change-name/`.
2. Complete the numbered stages in order.
3. Stop at every human gate.

## Outputs
- One human-editable change record under `changes/`.

## Human check
Read the stage output before allowing work to advance.
