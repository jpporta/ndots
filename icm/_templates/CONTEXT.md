# Templates — instantiate repeatable work
One job: hold blank, reusable starters for ICM records.

## Inputs
- Stable change process in `../CONTEXT.md`.

## Process
Copy a complete template folder; never begin a change record from an empty folder.

## Outputs
- A copied, renamed folder under `../changes/`.

## Human check
Confirm the copied change has a meaningful date and slug before investigation begins.
